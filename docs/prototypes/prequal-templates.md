## Scope
- **Persona:** GC Prequal Admin (Sam Whitaker in the mock). Low-frequency, high-consequence: defaults, progressive disclosure, a safe preview before publishing (PRD UX l. 779).
- **Decision these screens support:** what a subcontractor on a given trade and project size is asked for, which documents, thresholds, checks and knockouts apply, and how the score is weighted; and whether a template is safe to use (status, usage, what's missing).
- **Nav:** Preconstruction › Configure › Templates (D-13). `PrequalShell` now selects the tool whose rail lists the screen, so the Configure rail shows with Templates selected.
- **Data:** `src/modules/prequal/data/templates.js` (records, actions, rows, warnings) over `templateCatalog.js` (Linarc defaults: 12 sections with 73 default fields in the portal's ids and order, 24 document types, 14 thresholds, 6 weight sections, 7 knockout rules, 5 baselines, 10 custom field types). Eight templates: the six every request already names (now full definitions, with a custom field or document on four of them), a Draft (Federal / Secure Facility, weights 95%) and an Archived one. The shared `templates` name map is derived from the records, so no other screen changed. The session stores edited records under `templates`; Reset demo data clears them.

## Layout
**List:** Title Bar / Page “Templates” + New template; DataGrid list view, Template frozen left, action pinned right; columns Template · Version · Status · Trades · Project size · Project types · Documents · Checks · Knockouts · Used by · Last updated · Edit / Open; Filter panel with quick filters Active · Draft · Archived · In use.
**Builder:** Title Bar / Workflow (back, name, `v4` Chip, StatusChip; Duplicate · Archive / Restore; Draft saved), six-step ProgressStepper (every step reachable), 56rem column, footer Back / Cancel · Save draft · Next / Publish. Steps as in `screens.md` §10. Confirmations (Publish with who is affected, Archive, Leave without saving) in ModalV2.

## Update 2026-10-12 (review): custom sections and groups, reorder, filter, preview way back (D-32, F-071)

- **Preview as subcontractor** now carries a **Preview** status chip after the title and **Back to template editor** in place of Submit; it returns to the builder on the step the admin left (the builder remembers it in sessionStorage). The plain "Preview of … Nothing here is saved or sent." line stays above the content.
- **Custom sections.** Add custom section (below the last section) adds a section of the admin's own: a title input, a select for the navigation group it sits under (Company identity & compliance · Safety & insurance · Financial capacity · Experience & performance · Submit), its Required / Optional / Not asked select, and Remove section (while it has no fields). It gets the same requirements table and Add custom field. The portal nav, the overview, the tracking view and the review's Submission tab list it under the chosen group (`pillarsFor`).
- **Groups.** Add group on any section opens a Groups table (title · With a container / Heading only · field count). Custom fields are assigned to a group from the row editor's Group select (or the Group column); the portal renders the group's fields together under its heading, inside a hairline container when asked (FormV2 layout group with a `box-` id; `HeadingField` in `portalFields.js`).
- **Rolling up.** The row editor's **Section** select moves a custom field to another section (its group resets); so several custom fields can be gathered into any section, with or without a group container.
- **Reorder.** Custom rows carry a grip and can be dragged onto any row of their table to take its place (RepeatField `reorderable`, native drag and drop); the order is the portal's order.
- **Filter.** One SegmentedControl at the top of the step, **All** by default: Required · Optional · Not requested · Custom narrow every table ("n of m fields"); edits with a filter on merge back into the full lists. Each section's own state moved to a small select (F-071).

## Update 2026-10-12 (review 2): groups from inside the custom field editor; the editor scrolls into view

- **Grouping is an option of the custom field, not a separate flow.** The Groups table and Add group buttons are gone (the user found adding a group at the bottom and having the table appear at the top of the section confusing). In the custom field editor, **Put this field in a group** (a checkbox) reveals a **Group** select (the section's groups, or New group…); a chosen or new group reveals **Group title** and **Container** (heading only / with a container). Add or Save creates the new group or updates the chosen one; a group with no fields left is dropped. The section heading's count says "· 1 group".

The portal's row editors get the same behaviour.

## Update 2026-10-13: guided fixes from Review & publish

Asked by the user: a warning's link should land on the section and the item, with a marker and a note on what and how to fix, instead of a step to scroll and scan; the billing warning's link was dead.
- **Every warning says where, what and how** (`warningsFor` in `data/templates.js`): `target` (the step and the element key), `fix` (one sentence: "Configure the field and pick another evidence document, or set Drug-testing policy to Required or Optional under Documents.") and `go` (the action's own label: "Go to Requirements › Safety › Drug and alcohol program", "Go to Scoring & knockouts › Section weights", "Acknowledge below").
- **The action lands on the item** (`components/targetFocus.js`, mixed into every step): the builder switches step and sets `focus = { key, n }`; the step scrolls the element tagged `data-target="<key>"` (FormV2 fields by `data-field-id`) into the middle of the view and marks it with a warning tint for three seconds (`.is-targeted`, token variables). Targets: Basics fields, each section heading, each requirements row (`row:<section>:<field>`), each document row, the Documents block, the weights table, the bands, the checks list, and the Billing block on the same step (no navigation; the acknowledgement is the fix).
- **Rows carry the warning** (RepeatField `props.issues`): a warning marker (TriangleAlert, `icon/warning-low`) before the requirement opens a note under the row (InlineNotice: what is wrong, how to fix, Configure, Dismiss); arriving from Review & publish opens it. Section-level warnings (no title, no fields, an untitled group) are said under the section heading.
- **Verified** (`walk_warnings.mjs`): Federal draft → both warnings carry fixes and item labels → Acknowledge below marks Billing on the same step (864–916px of 1000) → ticking it removes the warning → Go to Scoring & knockouts › Section weights lands on the weights table. Electrical → Drug-testing policy set to Not requested → the Safety row shows a marker (no note) → Review & publish lists the warning with the fix → Go to Requirements › Safety › Drug and alcohol program lands on the row (495–576px) with the note open → Dismiss closes it, the marker reopens it. An empty custom section's warning lands on its heading with the fix. Other walks and the build pass.
